## Designed Systems — Build Phase Starting

**Design: COMPLETE · Architecture: LOCKED · Build: STARTING Q2 2026**

### [FundsLink Academy](https://github.com/KSDRILL-SA/fundslink-academy) — National Education Funding Platform
**Problem:** 342,000+ students excluded yearly due to fragmented bursary applications.
**Solution:** A unified platform aggregating all funding sources — bursaries, NSFAS, SETA, scholarships — with AI-powered eligibility matching.
**Stack:** Angular · FastAPI (Python) · PostgreSQL (Prisma) · LangChain · ChromaDB
**Status:** Design COMPLETE · Build STARTING Q2 2026 · Target Q3 2026

### [Maphophe Community System](https://github.com/KSDRILL-SA/maphophe-community-system) — Digital Governance for Villages
**Problem:** Rural villages have no digital infrastructure — announcements are verbal, requests get lost, decisions unrecorded.
**Solution:** Village-level governance with structured announcements, trackable service requests, community voting, and ward-level reporting.
**Stack:** Next.js · NextAuth.js · PostgreSQL (Prisma)
**Status:** Design COMPLETE · Build STARTING Q3 2026 · Target Q4 2026

### [KSDRILL Reserve Bank](https://github.com/KSDRILL-SA/ksdrill-reserve-bank) — Discipline-Driven Savings Banking
**Problem:** Most people save inconsistently — no enforced discipline, no goal visibility, no consequence.
**Solution:** Structured savings with enforced deposit schedules, real-time progress, interest tracking, and lock periods.
**Stack:** Angular · FastAPI (Python) · PostgreSQL · Redis · BullMQ
**Status:** Design COMPLETE · Build STARTING Q4 2026 · Target Q1 2027

### [SyncUp](https://github.com/KSDRILL-SA/syncup) — Creator Collaboration Platform
**Problem:** Creators waste time in unstructured DM negotiations. No standard process for pitching or formalising agreements.
**Solution:** Structured platform with template-based pitch flows, 10-message negotiation limit, and drop-based subscriptions.
**Stack:** Next.js · NextAuth.js · PostgreSQL · Redis · BullMQ
**Status:** Design COMPLETE · Build STARTING Q1 2027 · Target Q2 2027
